WRESTLING
"The oldest grappling sport, and no one has improved on the basics."

SNAPSHOT
  Origin:      Worldwide (ancient origins)
  Category:    Grappling
  Difficulty:  Intermediate
  Focus:       Takedowns, takedown defense, top control, escapes

WHAT IT IS
Wrestling is a grappling sport and martial art built on takedowns and control.
The aim is to take an opponent to the ground, keep them there, and either pin
them or win on points, while preventing the same being done to you. It
rewards strength, stamina, and relentless pressure, and it is one of the most
physically demanding martial arts.

HISTORY
Wrestling is among the oldest forms of combat sport, with traces in many early
cultures and a place in the ancient Olympic Games. The main styles practiced
today are folkstyle (common in North American schools), freestyle, and
Greco-Roman (which limits holds to the upper body). Wrestling has been an
Olympic sport for much of the modern Games' history, and it underpins much of
modern mixed martial arts.

WHAT TRAINING LOOKS LIKE
  - Hard conditioning: running, drilling, and strength work.
  - Stance and motion drills, then takedown entries such as single and double
    leg shots.
  - Drilling takedowns, sprawls, and escapes with a partner.
  - Live wrestling (rolling) on a mat, often in short, intense rounds.
  - No uniform jacket is used: practice is usually in a singlet or shorts and a
    shirt, with ear guards sometimes used.

CORE SKILLS
  Takedowns        Single legs, double legs, and trips that bring an opponent
                   to the mat.
  Takedown defense Sprawling and balance to stay on your feet.
  Top control      Riding, breaking down, and holding an opponent in place.
  Escapes          Standing up and getting out from underneath.
  Conditioning     Stamina and strength built through constant live work.

STRENGTHS
  - Teaches you to control where a fight goes, including staying on your feet.
  - Outstanding conditioning, strength, and mental toughness.
  - Practiced live at high intensity, so techniques are well tested.
  - Few techniques to learn, and they work without a uniform.

LIMITATIONS
  - No striking, and no submissions in most styles.
  - Physically demanding, with a high rate of sprains and strains.
  - Often offered mainly through schools and youth programs, so adult clubs can
    be harder to find in some areas.
  - Intense, high-pressure training can be a steep start for people new to
    physical contact.

SELF-DEFENSE VALUE
Very useful for avoiding being taken down, staying on your feet, and controlling
a single attacker. Combine it with striking and awareness for a more complete
approach.

GOOD FIT FOR
Athletic people who like hard physical training, and anyone who wants a strong
foundation for staying on their feet and controlling a fight.
